### Why Should You Hire Me?

When hiring for a position, I imagine that there are a vast number of candidates who may fit the job description, but not every one will fit the company. After all, the company is a team that works together to achieve a common goal. This includes a can-do attitude from each and every member as well as an alignment of mindsets that harmonize with the company's mission.

From my group projects, such as the Tax Credit Program Analysis by Region, to the coworkers I have had the distinct pleasure of working with, I formed an ability to see my role in any team and fit into that role to complete any task.
While many candidates a company receives are qualified for a given position, I am positive I am not only qualified, but will fit in the company culture.

The plethora of labs, data wrangling and analysis techniques, and high level math courses I have taken give me a strong foundation to contribute to any company. 
My open-mindedness and ability to take feedback to become a better employee allows me to strive to always be a better version of myself both in my work and to my peers.
